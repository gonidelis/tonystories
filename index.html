Music. Streets. People.
In post-production

Who does a
city belong to?

On the streets of New Orleans, musicians turn public space into a stage, a livelihood, and a place to belong.

The Souls of New Orleans follows the people behind the music, from the joy of a shared performance to the pressures that threaten their place in the city.

A film by Giannis Gonidelis

Stay curious.

Tony’s Stories is an independent documentary project founded by Giannis Gonidelis, a Greek filmmaker based in the San Francisco Bay Area.

Named in homage to Anthony Bourdain’s curiosity about people and places, it starts with a simple belief: everyone has a story worth listening to.
